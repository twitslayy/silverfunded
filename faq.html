Rules & trading

The daily loss limit is 3% of your current account balance, calculated fresh each day. "Floating" means it updates as your balance changes — so if your $1,000 account grows to $1,200, your daily limit becomes $36. If it drops to $980, the limit becomes $29.40. This means you have more room to manoeuvre as you grow.

Payouts

When you reach the 20% profit target and request a payout, 70% of the profits generated come to you. Silver Funded takes 30%. For example: you earn $200 profit on the $1,000 account — your payout is $140. There are no other deductions.
Once you hit the 20% profit target, contact Silver Funded support to initiate a payout request. You will need to provide proof of your account performance. Payout methods and processing times will be communicated through the support team.
