Profit target — 20%

To request a payout on a funded account, your account balance must reach or exceed 20% above the starting balance. This applies to both the $1,000 instant account and the $2,000 funded account.

$2,000 Account / Challenge
Target: $2,400 balance
That's a $400 gain.

There is no time limit to reach the profit target. Trade at your own pace.

Maximum loss — 6%

Your total account drawdown must never exceed 6% of the original starting balance at any point. If it does, the account is immediately closed. This is calculated from the opening balance, not the peak.

$1,000 Account
Minimum balance: $940
Max loss of $60 total.
$2,000 Account / Challenge
Minimum balance: $1,880
Max loss of $120 total.

Daily loss limit — 3% floating

You may not lose more than 3% of your current account balance in a single trading day (midnight to midnight UTC). This limit is floating, which means it is recalculated daily based on your current balance — it rises as you grow your account.

Example (floating): If your $1,000 account grows to $1,100, your daily loss limit for that day becomes 3% of $1,100 = $33. As your balance grows, your daily buffer grows with it.

Breaching the daily loss limit on any day results in account closure.

Profit split — 70% / 30%

When you request a payout after reaching the 20% profit target, you receive 70% of all profits generated. Silver Funded retains 30%. This applies to both the $1,000 instant funded account and any $2,000 funded account activated after passing the challenge.

Account closure conditions

An account is closed immediately if any of the following occurs:

✕ The total account loss exceeds 6% of the starting balance.
✕ The daily loss exceeds 3% of the current floating balance in a single trading day.
✕ Any evidence of rule manipulation, copy trading across Silver Funded accounts, or other prohibited practices.

All fees paid (the $1 entry fee and the $20 activation fee) are non-refundable regardless of account outcome.
